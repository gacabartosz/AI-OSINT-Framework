# Priority Tool Shortlist (A-List)

## Introduction

This document provides a **hard "A-list"** of high-priority OSINT tools that should always be considered first when conducting investigations. This is a non-exhaustive shortlist of default go-to tools across all major OSINT families.

**When to use this list**:
- At the start of every investigation (Phase 1 screening)
- When choosing which tools to prioritize from each family
- When time is limited and you need maximum impact

**Important**: These tools are NOT the only ones you can use, but they are the default high-priority candidates to check within each OSINT family whenever relevant to the case.

---

## PRIORITY TOOL SHORTLIST (ALWAYS CONSIDER WHEN RELEVANT)

### Core Search & Archive

**Search Engines:**
- **Google** (google.com)
- **Google Advanced Search** (google.com/advanced_search)
- **Bing** (bing.com)
- **DuckDuckGo** (duckduckgo.com)
- **Yandex** (yandex.com) – especially for images and people
- **StartPage** (startpage.com) – anonymous Google proxy

**Web Archives:**
- **Wayback Machine** (web.archive.org)
- **Archive.is / Archive.today** (archive.ph)
- **Cached Pages / Webcache** (Google Cache, Bing Cache)

**Why prioritize**: These are universal starting points for any investigation. Google and Wayback Machine alone can answer 50%+ of OSINT questions.

---

### People, Identity & Contact Information

**People Search Aggregators:**
- **Pipl** (pipl.com) – deep people search, global coverage
- **Spokeo** (spokeo.com) – US-focused, comprehensive
- **PeekYou** (peekyou.com) – social media aggregator
- **Radaris** (radaris.com) – free basic searches
- **ZoomInfo** (zoominfo.com) – B2B professional contacts
- **411 / Addresses** (411.com, addresses.com) – US-style people directories
- **WhitePages** (whitepages.com) – phone and address lookups

**Phone Number Tools:**
- **Truecaller** (truecaller.com) – caller ID and spam detection
- **Numberway** (numberway.com) – reverse phone lookup
- **Carrier lookup tools** (freecarrierlookup.com) – identify carrier/type

**Email Tools:**
- **HaveIBeenPwned** (haveibeenpwned.com) – breach exposure check
- **Dehashed** (dehashed.com) – leaked credentials search
- **MailTester** (mailtester.com) – email validation
- **Hunter.io** (hunter.io) – find emails by domain pattern

**Why prioritize**: People search is the most common OSINT task. These tools cover 90% of use cases and offer the best ROI.

---

### Social Media & Communities

**Multi-Network Search:**
- **Social Searcher** (social-searcher.com) – search across multiple platforms
- **Social Mention** (socialmention.com) – social media monitoring

**Facebook:**
- **Facebook ID / Graph Search tools** (public data only)
- **Facebook Advanced Search** (manual, within Facebook)
- **IntelTechniques Facebook Tools** (custom search pages)

**Twitter/X:**
- **Twitter Advanced Search** (twitter.com/search-advanced)
- **TweetDeck** (tweetdeck.twitter.com) – manage feeds
- **TweetBeaver** (tweetbeaver.com) – analytics
- **Topic & timeline tools** (Twitonomy, Botometer)

**Reddit:**
- **Pushshift Reddit Search** (redditsearch.io)
- **Reddit Investigator** (redditinvestigator.com)
- **Comment search** (camas.unddit.com)

**Forums & Blogs:**
- **Forum / blog search engines** (BoardReader, Google CSE for forums)
- **Tapatalk** (tapatalk.com) – forum aggregator

**Why prioritize**: Social media is where people reveal the most about themselves. These tools surface public posts, opinions, and connections.

---

### Companies, Business & Finance

**Corporate Registries:**
- **OpenCorporates** (opencorporates.com) – global company database
- **Company-check tools** (varies by jurisdiction)
- **Secretary of State databases** (for US states)
- **Companies House** (companieshouse.gov.uk) – UK

**Business Reputation & Reviews:**
- **Better Business Bureau** (bbb.org) – US business ratings
- **Glassdoor** (glassdoor.com) – employee reviews and salaries
- **Trustpilot** (trustpilot.com) – customer reviews

**Financial Filings:**
- **SEC.gov / EDGAR** (sec.gov/edgar) – US public company filings (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K)
- **State corporate registries** – annual reports, officer changes
- **Crunchbase** (crunchbase.com) – startup funding data

**Business Credit & Banking:**
- **Business credit reports** (Dun & Bradstreet, Experian Business)
- **Bank & routing number search** (where publicly available)

**Why prioritize**: Corporate due diligence requires authoritative sources. These are the most reliable and comprehensive.

---

### Domain, IP & Infrastructure

**WHOIS & DNS:**
- **WHOIS Lookup** (whois.domaintools.com, who.is)
- **ViewDNS** (viewdns.info) – comprehensive DNS tools
- **DNS tools** (dnsdumpster.com, mxtoolbox.com)

**Infrastructure Profiling:**
- **Shodan** (shodan.io) – search for internet-connected devices
- **Censys** (censys.io) – certificate and service search
- **RiskIQ / PassiveTotal** (passivetotal.org) – infrastructure intelligence
- **SecurityTrails** (securitytrails.com) – DNS history

**IP & Hosting:**
- **IP geolocation tools** (iplocation.net, ipinfo.io)
- **Hosting lookup** (who hosts this, reverse IP)

**Why prioritize**: Domain and IP intel is critical for website/infrastructure investigations. Shodan and Censys reveal security posture.

---

### Documents & Data Leaks

**Academic & Patents:**
- **Google Scholar** (scholar.google.com) – academic papers
- **Google Patent Search** (patents.google.com)
- **USPTO** (uspto.gov) – US patents

**Document Search:**
- **PDF Drive** (pdfdrive.com) – PDF search engine
- **SlideShare** (slideshare.net) – presentations
- **Document search engines** (custom Google CSEs for filetypes)

**Data Leaks:**
- **Pastebin** (pastebin.com) – manual searches
- **Paste-monitoring tools** (psbdmp.ws)
- **Public leak archives** (IntelX, BreachForums – access ethically)

**Why prioritize**: Documents often contain metadata, credentials, or proprietary info. Leaks expose security risks.

---

### Images, Video, Geolocation & Metadata

**Reverse Image Search:**
- **Google Images** (images.google.com)
- **Yandex Images** (yandex.com/images) – superior for faces
- **TinEye** (tineye.com) – finds oldest instances

**Metadata & Forensics:**
- **FotoForensics** (fotoforensics.com) – detect image manipulation
- **EXIF viewers** (Jeffrey's EXIF Viewer, exifdata.com)
- **Metadata analyzers** (metadata2go.com)

**Maps & Satellite:**
- **Google Maps / Street View** (maps.google.com)
- **OpenStreetMap** (openstreetmap.org)
- **Mapbox** (mapbox.com)
- **Zoom Earth** (zoom.earth) – satellite imagery
- **Google Earth** (earth.google.com) – historical imagery

**Webcams:**
- **Webcam aggregators** (EarthCam, Windy Webcams)

**Why prioritize**: Images reveal location, time, and context. Metadata often contains GPS coordinates and timestamps.

---

### Government, Courts, Crime & Sanctions

**Court Records:**
- **Court records directories** (varies by jurisdiction)
- **PACER-style indexes** (pacer.gov for US federal courts, where user has legal access)
- **CourtListener** (courtlistener.com) – free legal opinions
- **State court websites**

**Sanctions & Watchlists:**
- **OFAC Sanctions List** (treasury.gov/ofac) – US sanctions
- **EU Sanctions Map** (sanctionsmap.eu)
- **UN Sanctions** (un.org/securitycouncil/sanctions)
- **PEP lists** (Politically Exposed Persons databases)

**Crime & Fraud:**
- **Crime / scam / fraud alert databases** (BBB Scam Tracker, FTC complaints)
- **"Most wanted" lists** (FBI, Interpol)
- **Sex offender registries** (where legally accessible)

**Why prioritize**: Legal and sanctions checks are critical for compliance and risk assessment. Always check OFAC before international business.

---

### Property, Vehicles, Vessels, Aviation

**Property (Real Estate):**
- **Property / assessor databases** (county-level in US)
- **Zillow** (zillow.com) – US property values
- **Realtor.com** (realtor.com)
- **PropertyShark** (propertyshark.com)

**Vehicles:**
- **VIN / vehicle lookup** (vehiclehistory.gov, Carfax, AutoCheck)
- **License plate lookup** (where legal – varies by jurisdiction)

**Vessels:**
- **Vessel trackers** (MarineTraffic, VesselFinder)
- **IMO number lookup** (maritime databases)

**Aviation:**
- **Flight trackers** (FlightAware, FlightRadar24)
- **FAA Registry** (registry.faa.gov) – US aircraft ownership

**Why prioritize**: Asset searches and tracking are common in investigations. These tools provide real-time and historical data.

## How to Use the A-List

### During Phase 1 (Quick Screening)
- **Always start with**: Google, social media search, WHOIS (if domain), people aggregators
- **Red flag check**: OFAC, HaveIBeenPwned, BBB, court records

### During Phase 2 (Deep Dive)
- Use A-list tools as your **first choice** within each relevant family
- Example: For people search, start with Pipl/Spokeo before trying lesser-known tools
- Example: For images, start with Yandex Images before niche reverse search engines

### When Time is Limited
- **Stick to A-list only**: These tools deliver 80% of results in 20% of time
- Skip experimental or niche tools

### When A-List Fails
- **Then** explore secondary tools from the full OSINT families list (see `03_osint_tool_families.md`)
- Document why A-list didn't work (helps refine approach for future cases)

## Maintenance & Updates

**This A-list is current as of 2024 but will evolve.** Tools change, new ones emerge, old ones shut down.

### How to stay current:
- Check for tool status updates quarterly
- Follow OSINT communities (Reddit r/OSINT, Twitter #OSINT)
- Test new tools before adding to A-list
- Remove deprecated or unreliable tools

### Criteria for A-list inclusion:
- **Reliability**: Consistently delivers accurate results
- **Coverage**: Broad applicability (not too niche)
- **Legality**: 100% legal and ToS-compliant
- **Accessibility**: Free tier or reasonable cost
- **Community trust**: Widely used by OSINT professionals

---

## Legal & Ethical Reminders

Even when using A-list tools:
- **Respect Terms of Service** of each platform
- **Comply with GDPR, CCPA, FCRA** where applicable
- **Only access public data** – no authentication bypass
- **Document sources** in your investigation log
- **Assess reliability** – even A-list tools can have stale or incorrect data

**When in doubt, verify across 3+ independent sources.**
